Privacy Policy — Naam Jap Counter

Last updated: 2 July 2026 · App: Naam Jap Counter: Mantra Mala Counter (com.japacounter.app)

Naam Jap Counter ("the app", "we", "us") is a mantra and mala counting app. We respect your privacy and designed the app to work with as little personal data as possible. You can use Naam Jap Counter fully offline, and signing in is always optional. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information (optional): If you choose to Sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile identifier. This is used only to save your chanting progress and show your name on the leaderboard, synced across your devices. If you never sign in, we do not collect this.
  • Practice data (optional): If you are signed in, your japa counts, streaks, and progress are stored so they can sync and appear on the weekly leaderboard. If you are not signed in, this data stays only on your device.
  • Microphone / voice: When you use hands-free Voice Japa, the app accesses your microphone only to count your chanting in real time. The audio is streamed to your device's built-in speech-recognition service to recognise the mantra, and is then discarded. The app does not record, save, or upload your voice. See section 3.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not record or store audio files of your voice.
  • This version contains no advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or files.

3. Speech recognition

To count your chanting hands-free, Naam Jap Counter uses your device's built-in speech-recognition capability (provided by your operating system, e.g. Google on Android). Depending on your device settings, audio may be processed by that OS service to convert speech to text, which the app then uses solely to increment your count. This processing is governed by your device/OS provider's own privacy policy. Naam Jap Counter itself does not retain the audio or the recognised text.

4. How we use information

  • To count and display your japa and mala progress.
  • To provide optional cloud sync and the weekly leaderboard when you sign in.
  • To operate optional community/group features you choose to join.

5. Data sharing

We use Google Sign-In and Google/Firebase services to authenticate you and store synced progress if you sign in. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms. We do not share your personal data with any other third parties for their own marketing.

6. Data retention & deletion

Local data (when not signed in) lives only on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data. If you signed in and want your synced account data deleted, sign out to clear local data, or email us at the address below and we will delete your associated records.

7. Children

Naam Jap Counter is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond the optional account sign-in described above.

8. Security

Data transmitted for sign-in and sync is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
